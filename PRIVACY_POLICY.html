Privacy Policy for MyStickerApp

Last updated: May 2024 / Current Version

Welcome to MyStickerApp ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, device permissions, third-party advertising, and data safety.

Summary: MyStickerApp does not sell your personal data. Photo editing and background removal are processed directly on your device. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may collect anonymous device identifiers to serve ads.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

MyStickerApp does not require user registration or account creation to use its primary features. We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, or personal identity unless directly provided by you for support inquiries.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

To provide custom sticker creation tools, MyStickerApp requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera Access (CAMERA): Required if you choose to take a photo inside the app to turn it into a sticker. Photos captured using your camera are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any cloud server or third party.
  • Storage & Media Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to allow you to select images from your gallery to create stickers and to save your created sticker packs directly to your device storage or export them to messaging applications (such as WhatsApp).

3. Image Processing & AI Technology

MyStickerApp uses on-device artificial intelligence (TensorFlow Lite / MediaPipe) for automated background removal and sticker cropping. All image processing occurs 100% locally on your smartphone. Your photos are never transmitted over the internet for processing.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services (AdMob)

MyStickerApp uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • App performance and crash logs

This information is used by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and analyze ad metrics. You can learn more about how Google handles user data by reading the Google Privacy Policy.

5. WhatsApp & Third-Party Integrations

When you export stickers to third-party messaging apps (such as WhatsApp), the stickers are transferred directly between applications on your device using standard Android ContentProvider protocols. No data is stored or intercepted by us during this process.

6. Children's Privacy

MyStickerApp does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, physical, and technical measures to safeguard your app experience. Because your sticker projects remain locally on your device, you maintain full control over your content.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
